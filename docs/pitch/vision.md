# Pitch — Vision

> Where Launchpad is going.

**Status:** Current plan · **Updated:** 2026-09-22

## Decision

Launchpad should become a controlled AI software-development organisation that you can run from anywhere. You define the product and make the decisions; specialist agents and developers execute; and you intervene only when needed, from a browser, a phone and, later, by voice.
