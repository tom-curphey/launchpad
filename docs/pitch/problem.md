# Pitch — Problem

> The problem Launchpad solves.

**Status:** Decided · **Updated:** 2026-09-22

## Decision

AI coding systems are increasingly good at *doing work*, but they lack a reliable understanding of why the work exists, what the product is meant to be, what's already decided, what's in scope, what must stay unchanged, how work relates, when to ask a human, who owns the next action, and whether the result meets the intended outcome.

The result is that humans keep prompting, keep asking what's next, keep reconstructing context, and have to stay at their machines to babysit agents.
