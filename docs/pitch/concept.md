# Pitch — Concept

> Launchpad's core concept in one statement.

**Status:** Decided · **Updated:** 2026-09-22

## Decision

**Launchpad takes a product from idea to live.** It is the product-development layer that connects product thinking to AI and human execution: Pitch → Assemble → Deploy → Live.
