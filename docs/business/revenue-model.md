# Business — Revenue Model

> How Launchpad makes money, and what it deliberately doesn't sell.

**Status:** Current plan · **Updated:** 2026-09-22

## Decision

1. **Installable and customer-controlled.** Customers install Launchpad and connect their own AI provider, GitHub, Linear, Supabase and infrastructure.
2. **Bring your own AI.** Launchpad doesn't resell AI tokens. Customers pay their model provider directly, which keeps AI billing separate from Launchpad's value.
3. **What Launchpad sells:** the product-development system, interface and orchestration. That covers the Product Definition, the decision system, planning, Workflow, agent orchestration, the cross-project dashboard, human intervention, development visibility, review, UAT, deployment visibility, and product-to-code traceability.
