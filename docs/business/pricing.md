# Business — Pricing

> How Launchpad is priced.

**Status:** Under evaluation · **Updated:** 2026-09-22

## Decision

1. **Not a current focus.** Pricing will be decided later.
2. **Never per user.** Launchpad will never charge per seat. Companies add as many GitHub users as they like.
3. **Bring your own AI.** AI usage is paid by the company to its provider, never through Launchpad. See [Revenue Model](revenue-model.md).
