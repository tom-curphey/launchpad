# Design — Responsive

> How the dashboard works on a phone.

**Status:** Decided · **Updated:** 2026-09-22

## Decision

1. **Mobile-first for intervention.** On a phone the first screen answers: what needs me, what's running, what finished, what failed, and what decision is required.
2. **One-tap actions:** Approve, Reject, Answer, Pause, Resume, Reassign, View context, View PR.
3. **Readable context:** a blocked item shows the question, the ticket, and the relevant decision on one screen.
4. Deep editing, such as the Workflow graph or large Draft Ticket boards, may be desktop-first.
